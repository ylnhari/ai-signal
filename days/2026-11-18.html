OpenAI launches GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, cutting Astra's price 20%

OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra, its new frontier model, and GPT-6.2 Sol, a cheaper everyday model, on 17 November. Astra's API price falls to $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens, 20% below GPT-6 Astra, with cached input at $0.40; Sol keeps GPT-6.1 Sol's prices. OpenAI reports new highs for Astra on Agents' Last Exam, Terminal-Bench-Science and the Vals Science Index, and says Sol lands within 0.5 points of Astra on FrontierCode 2.0 at about a fifth of the cost. These are the vendor's own figures; early hands-on reaction is mixed, with Astra reported behind Claude Fable 5.2 on some benchmarks. Both models meet OpenAI's "Critical" cybersecurity threshold and ship with tighter safeguards for security work.

NVIDIA posts $115.8 billion quarter and guides to about $130 billion

NVIDIA reported third-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103% from a year earlier, with Data Center revenue of $107.6 billion, up 110%. It guided the 14-week fourth quarter to about $130 billion, plus or minus 3%, assuming no Data Center compute revenue from China. The company says Vera Rubin NVL72 racks are shipping, calling it the fastest product ramp in its history, with systems running at CoreWeave, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, Oracle Cloud Infrastructure and Nebius.

Microsoft ships MAI Code 2 and Code 2 Flash into GitHub Copilot

Microsoft announced two in-house coding models at Ignite on 17 November, both available in GitHub Copilot, with Code 2 Flash in limited preview in Microsoft Foundry. Microsoft says Code 2 averages 98% of GPT-6.1 Sol's and 96% of Claude Opus 5.5's scores across four coding benchmarks, at a stated 40% and 5% of their costs respectively, shown at a 50% promotional price. No dollar prices or open weights are given, and the benchmark figures are Microsoft's own.

Unsloth adds computer use and a new model picker

Unsloth's desktop app now lets local vision models and connected providers see the screen and drive the mouse and keyboard on macOS, Windows and Linux. The feature is in beta, off by default, and asks for approval before the first action. The release also adds MiniMax-H3-Image in GGUF, FP8 and INT8 with LoRA training, MCP integrations for Unity, Unreal, Godot, Blender and Roblox, automatic conversation compaction, and reported 1.4–2.6x faster Gemma 4 12B training.

NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang resigns; CFO Colette Kress named interim CEO

NVIDIA's board announced on November 12 that Jensen Huang has resigned as president, CEO and director, effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress becomes interim CEO while keeping her finance role, and Harvey C. Jones, a former Synopsys CEO, becomes the company's first board chair. The release gives no reason and no new financial guidance. NVIDIA says it will still report third-quarter fiscal 2027 results on November 17, and a search for a permanent CEO, internal or external, is under way. It follows the board's November 10 statement on an independent investigation, though the release does not connect the two.

Gemini 4 Hydrogen: Google's cheaper "daily driver" tier

Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen on November 12 as the fast, low-cost tier below Carbon and Argon, aimed at coding and agentic work. It costs $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens, with a flat 1M-token context and a new xhigh thinking level. Google reports an Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index of 51 at default (Argon: 50) and 57% on Terminal-Bench 4.1, against 18.2% for Gemini 3.8 Flash; these are the company's own figures, with limited methodology. It is on the Gemini API and AI Studio, in Antigravity and Search, and appears on OpenRouter; some surfaces need a paid plan.

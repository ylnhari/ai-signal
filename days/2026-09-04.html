GPT-6 Astra: OpenAI's first model to cross the "Critical" cyber-capability line

OpenAI began rolling out GPT-6 Astra today, its new flagship model, calling it "the world's most intelligent and aligned model." OpenAI's own benchmark claims: 98% on FrontierMath Tier 4, 99.9% on ARC-AGI-3, and a 47% cut in time-per-task on computer-use benchmarks versus GPT-5.6 Sol. More consequential than the scores: OpenAI says Astra is the first model to cross the "Critical" cybersecurity-capability threshold under its own Preparedness Framework — meaning it can find and exploit previously unknown vulnerabilities with little human guidance — and paired the launch with a same-day $1 billion "Daybreak for Frontline Defenders" program subsidizing AI-driven cyber-defense tools for under-resourced defenders (water utilities, electric grids, local governments). Availability starts today with a limited set of organizations, expanding over the coming days to all ChatGPT tiers, the OpenAI API (gpt-6-astra), and AWS Bedrock, at $10/$50 per million input/output tokens standard (Fast mode: 2x the speed at 2x the price).

Nvidia to acquire Hugging Face for ~$12.9B

Nvidia agreed to acquire Hugging Face for roughly $12.9 billion, announced by Jensen Huang on 2026-09-03 — one of the largest acquisitions yet in AI infrastructure. Both companies say Hugging Face will stay an independent, multi-cloud, hardware-agnostic platform: developers keep choosing their own models, frameworks, clouds, and inference providers, with no stated requirement to run on Nvidia hardware. Nvidia frames the deal as building on its existing role as Hugging Face's largest single contributor of open models and datasets (500+ models, 250+ datasets released there to date). The announcement gives no closing date or regulatory-approval detail.

OpenAI posts preprints claiming a matrix-multiplication exponent of 9/4 and a VP–VNP separation

OpenAI published two theory preprints on its GitHub in as many days. The first, dated November 24, is titled "The matrix multiplication exponent is nine-fourths", a claim that would settle how fast matrices can be multiplied in theory. The second, dated November 23, claims a lower bound of exp(Ω((log n)^{5/4})) on border arithmetic circuit size for an explicit VNP family over every field, and ships a Lean proof of the exact VP–VNP separation. Both are self-published and not peer reviewed, so neither result is confirmed yet; the matrix paper is a PDF that could not be read here, so its claim rests on its title.

Anthropic shares close below their $83 IPO price on the fourth trading day

Anthropic fell 6.5% to close at $81.91 on Tuesday, below its $83 IPO price for the first time, in a broader pullback in AI-exposed stocks. Bloomberg puts the market value at about $1.5 trillion at that close; a Dow Jones piece reported a midday low of $75.61, which would be under $1 trillion on its share-count math. The two outlets give different intraday figures and market values, so treat the exact levels as unsettled. The stock rose 17% on its debut last Thursday and fell 8.5% on Friday.

Inkling 2 Mini gets fast local runtimes and fine-tuning support

Unsloth now fine-tunes Inkling 2 Mini 1.8–3.1x faster with 50% less VRAM using LoRA or QLoRA. It fits on Kaggle's free 2x T4 at about 26 s per step but not on a single 15 GB T4, and a fine-tuned model exports to a 17.5 GB q4_k_m GGUF. Ollama 0.40.18 adds faster Metal decode, prefill and draft rounds for Inkling, and llama.cpp builds b12696 onward add the Inkling 2 vision-encoder fix. The practical effect is that the new open-weights model is now usable for local inference and cheap tuning within a week of release.

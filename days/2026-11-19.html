Google ships Gemini 4 Neon, the mid-tier of its Gemini 4 family

Google released Gemini 4 Neon on 2026-11-18, positioned between Carbon (hardest long-horizon work) and Hydrogen (fast everyday tasks). It costs $3 per million input tokens, $0.15 cached input and $15 per million output tokens, with a 1M-token context window and four thinking levels (default "high", "xhigh" for the hardest work). It is available in the paid Gemini API (AI Studio, Android Studio), Gemini for business, Antigravity and the Gemini app for Pro and Ultra subscribers; a cyber variant is limited to vetted defenders. Google reports Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index #3 (61 at xhigh) and Coding Agent Index #1 (58.6), Terminal-Bench 4.1 at 65.2% and SEC-bench Pro at 90.5% — vendor-reported figures, not independently reproduced.

Anthropic prices its IPO at $83 a share

Anthropic priced its initial public offering on 2026-11-18: 980,000,000 Class A shares at $83.00 (956,351,354 from the company, 23,648,646 from selling stockholders), roughly $81.3 billion in total and about $79.4 billion to Anthropic by our arithmetic from the stated figures. Microsoft unveils MAI Code 2 and six other in-house models

At Ignite, Microsoft announced seven models: MAI Code 2 and Code 2 Flash (in GitHub Copilot), MAI Image 2.7, MAI Realtime 1 (from $0.01 per audio minute), MAI Cyber 2 Flash, Dragon Copilot Transcribe and Decision 1.1. Microsoft claims MAI Code 2 reaches 98% of GPT-6.1 Sol's and 96% of Claude Opus 5.5's coding performance at roughly 40% and 5% of their prices; all comparisons are Microsoft's own and unverified. It also announced the Maia 200 accelerator, claiming 1.7x more tokens per watt.

Google opens Genie 4, its interactive world model, to Ultra subscribers

Genie 4 is rolling out to eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers via Project Genie, with a waitlist for Pro. Google calls it its most realistic and interactive world model: users can edit a world mid-session by text prompt and get real-time native audio. Genie 3 stays selectable, now running up to 3 minutes per generation. A Project Genie agent builds worlds from conversation, and a community gallery allows remixing. Google lists camera latency, complex physics and long-term scene permanence as unsolved; outputs carry SynthID watermarks.

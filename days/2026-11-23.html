Thinking Machines releases Inkling 2 Mini, a 26B looped model for laptops

Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling 2 Mini with open weights on 2026-11-20. It is a 26.4B-parameter mixture-of-experts model (3.4B active, 5.4B weight activations per token) that runs its middle layers three times per token, so a small model gets deeper reasoning without longer written chains of thought. Weights are NVFP4/MXFP8, about 20GB in memory, so it fits a 48GB MacBook Pro; the lab claims the lead among open models under 100B parameters on DeepSWE v1.1 (48.2%) and Vals Index v2.2 (46.5%), vendor-reported and with evaluation setups that vary by model. Hugging Face's early hands-on read puts it at least on par with Qwen 3.8 27B and calls for more validation. Day-one support landed in Transformers v5.24.0, llama.cpp, MLX, SGLang and Unsloth, Ollama (Apple silicon only at first), and it is live on OpenRouter and Tinker. A research variant with a variable number of loop passes ships alongside.

Anthropic's IPO closes at $93.5 billion as an AI sell-off drags the Nasdaq to a 2026 low

Anthropic announced on 2026-11-20 the closing of its IPO: 1.127 billion Class A shares at $83 each, including the underwriters' full over-allotment, for about $93.5 billion gross ($91.6 billion to the company). Shares began trading on Nasdaq as ANT on 2026-11-19. A concurrent private placement sold about $26.0 billion of Class N shares to AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, NVIDIA and TSMC, bringing the total raised to about $119.5 billion. On the second trading day the Guardian reports the stock was worth hardly more than its listing price while the Nasdaq Composite erased all of its 2026 gains in a broad AI-driven sell-off.

OpenAI's MedChemBench tests whether models can reason like medicinal chemists

OpenAI introduced MedChemBench on 2026-11-20: 99 problems drawn from 117 papers and patents, covering molecule reconstruction, scientific reasoning, compound ranking and property prediction. At the xhigh reasoning setting GPT-6.2 Astra scored 84.5% of available credit, against 83.3% for GPT-6 Astra, 82.0% for GPT-6.1 Sol and 58.2% for GPT-5.6 Sol. These are OpenAI's own results on its own benchmark, and the post says selecting compounds and predicting numerical properties were the hardest parts.
